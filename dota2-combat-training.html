How the Product Works

Dota 2: Combat Training is an in-game training mode that teaches new players core combat mechanics through structured, progressive scenarios.

Players enter guided combat scenarios with contextual tips

›

Players practice mechanics in a low-stakes environment

›

Players unlock harder scenarios as skills improve

The Problem

New players faced a steep learning curve and churned early because the game offered no structured way to learn combat fundamentals.
